Privacy

Reefcalm is designed with no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

The app plans to use approximate location only to fetch local weather via Apple WeatherKit. That location is for the weather request. The app is not designed to use it for anything else.

Weather in the app will be provided by Apple Weather.
