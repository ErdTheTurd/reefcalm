Reefcalm

A calm aquarium for your Apple TV.

What it does

  • A living animated aquarium

    Original fish, light, and bubbles, moving slowly across the screen.

  • An elegant clock

    The time, set quietly into the picture.

  • Local weather at a glance

    The weather where you are, provided by Apple Weather.

Calm and low power by design

Reefcalm is meant to stay on the screen without asking for attention. Motion stays slow, the artwork is original, and little is shown at once, so the television can remain a quiet, low-power presence in the room.

Privacy

Reefcalm is designed with no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. The app plans to use approximate location only to fetch local weather via Apple WeatherKit.
