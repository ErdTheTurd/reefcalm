Reefcalm

Fish swim on your TV.

An aquarium for the living room. The time sits in the corner. Weather is optional, and comes from Apple Weather when you want it.

Coming soon to Apple TV.

What it does

On the main screen, and in Settings.

  • Fish

    A few fish swim across the television. They were drawn for Reefcalm, and they move slowly, among kelp and light on the sand.

  • Time

    Large, thin type in the corner, with the date under it. You can follow the system format, or switch to 12-hour or 24-hour in Settings.

  • Weather

    Optional. If you turn it on, Reefcalm fetches current conditions from Apple Weather. You can use this Apple TV’s location, or type a city or ZIP.

  • Remote

    After setup, press Play/Pause on the Siri Remote to open Settings. Press Menu, or choose Done, to go back to the aquarium.

From the Apple TV

Screens from the app, captured in the Apple TV simulator at 1920×1080.

Settings stay short

Clock, weather, and location. You can use this Apple TV’s location, or enter a city or ZIP. Location is only used to fetch local conditions from Apple Weather.

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, and no tracking. If you turn weather on, Reefcalm uses the Apple TV’s location, or a city or ZIP you type, only to fetch current conditions from Apple Weather.
