Reefcalm

Fish swim on your TV.

The time sits in the corner. Today’s temperature and conditions, for where you are. Made to sip power, so it can run all evening.

Coming soon to Apple TV.

What it does

On the main screen.

  • Fish

    A few fish swim across the television. They were drawn for Reefcalm, and they move slowly.

  • Time

    Large, thin type in the corner, with the date under it.

  • Weather

    Today’s temperature and conditions, for where you are. Supplied by Apple Weather.

  • Power

    Made to sip power, so it can run all evening.

Settings stay short

Clock, weather, and location. Location is approximate, and it is only there so the weather is for where you are.

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, and no tracking. The app plans to use approximate location only to fetch local weather via Apple WeatherKit.
