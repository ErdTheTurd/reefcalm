Support

For help with Reefcalm on Apple TV, email erdunn706@gmail.com.

If something is not working, it helps to say which Apple TV you are using, and what you see on the screen. If the question is about weather, mention whether you chose this Apple TV’s location or a city or ZIP.

A few things people ask

Weather is optional. You can skip it during onboarding, or turn it off in Settings. Weather comes from Apple Weather. If a request cannot be completed, the aquarium shows a short status, such as “Weather unavailable.”

If you do not want to use the Apple TV’s location, choose “Enter a city or ZIP” during onboarding, or switch to City or ZIP in Settings.

After onboarding, press Play/Pause on the Siri Remote to open Settings. Press Menu, or choose Done, to return to the aquarium.

The privacy policy explains how location and weather work.
